我深怕自己本非美玉，故而不敢加以刻苦琢磨，却又半信自己是块美玉， 故又不肯庸庸碌碌，与瓦砾为伍。

我经由光阴，经由山水，经由乡村和城市，同样我也经由别人， 经由一切他者以及由之引生的思绪和梦想而走成了我。 那路途中的一切，有些与我擦肩而过从此天各一方， 有些便永久驻进我的心魂，雕琢我，塑造我，锤炼我，融入我而成为我。

——史铁生《病隙碎笔》

I deeply fear that l am not a beautiful jade, hence l dare not diligently carve and polish myself. Yet, half-believing that l am indeed a precious jade, l refuse to live a mundane life, mingling with rubble.

"I have walked through time, through mountains and rivers, through villages and cities; in the same way, I have walked through other people, through all other beings and the thoughts and dreams they evoked, to become who I am today. Everything along that journey—some brushed past me and drifted away to the ends of the earth, while others permanently settled into my soul, carving, shaping, tempering, and merging into me to become a part of who I am."

-- Shi Tiesheng
